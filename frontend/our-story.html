Our Story

The Inspiration Behind GETNOTIFY

The Problem We Faced

It was a typical Monday morning when we received a heartbreaking message from a student named Rahul. He had been preparing for the JEE Main exam for over two years, spending countless hours studying, attending coaching classes, and solving practice papers. His dream was to get into a top engineering college, and he was confident about his preparation.

But there was one thing Rahul missed—the exam form submission deadline.

Rahul's Story

Rahul, like thousands of other students, was juggling multiple responsibilities. Between his school classes, coaching sessions, and self-study, he simply didn't have the time to check the NTA website every day for updates. He had checked the website a month ago and saw that the exam dates were announced, but he didn't realize that the form submission deadline was approaching.

He had set a reminder on his phone, but it got lost among dozens of other notifications. He was focused on his studies, confident that he would remember to fill the form when the time came. But life got busy, and the deadline passed without him even realizing it.

"I had been preparing for this exam for two years. I knew the syllabus inside out. I had solved thousands of practice questions. But I missed the form submission deadline because I didn't get notified about it. All my hard work felt wasted."

When Rahul finally checked the website, he saw that the deadline had passed three days ago. There was no way to submit the form now. His dream of appearing for JEE Main that year was over. He would have to wait another year, losing precious time and momentum.

The Realization

Rahul's story is not unique. Every year, thousands of students miss critical exam notifications, form submission deadlines, admit card releases, and result announcements simply because they don't have a reliable way to stay informed.

The problem is clear:

  • Students have to check multiple websites daily (CBSE, NTA, UPSC, state boards, etc.)
  • Official websites don't always send email notifications
  • Important announcements can get buried in social media feeds
  • Students are busy with studies and can't monitor websites constantly
  • Missing a single notification can cost a student an entire year

Our Solution

This is where GETNOTIFY comes in. We asked ourselves: "What if there was a single platform that monitored all major exam boards and sent instant notifications to students?"

We built GETNOTIFY to solve exactly this problem:

  • 24/7 Monitoring: Our automated system continuously monitors official websites of all major exam boards
  • Instant Notifications: As soon as a new notification is published, we capture it and make it available on our platform
  • One Centralized Place: Instead of checking 10+ websites, students can check one platform
  • Never Miss Anything: With our comprehensive coverage, students can be confident they won't miss important updates
  • Easy to Use: Simple, clean interface that makes finding information effortless

Our Mission

GETNOTIFY was created with one simple mission: to ensure that no student ever misses an important exam notification again.

We believe that every student deserves equal access to information. Your success shouldn't depend on whether you remembered to check a website on a particular day. With GETNOTIFY, you can focus on what matters most—your preparation—while we handle the rest.

We're here to make sure that stories like Rahul's become a thing of the past. Your dreams are important, and we're committed to helping you achieve them by keeping you informed every step of the way.
