About Us

Your Gateway to Educational Updates

Who We Are

GETNOTIFY is a dedicated educational notification platform designed to help students stay informed about critical exam updates, announcements, and deadlines. We understand the challenges students face in keeping track of multiple exam boards, application deadlines, and result announcements.

Our mission is simple: to ensure that no student misses an important exam notification, application deadline, or result announcement due to lack of information.

What We Do

We continuously monitor official websites of major examination boards and educational authorities across India, including:

  • National Level: CBSE, NTA, UPSC, JEE, NEET, GATE, ISRO
  • State Level: MPSC, KSEAB, KEA, MHT-CET
  • Railway Boards: RRB Ahmedabad, RRB Kolkata, RRB Mumbai

We aggregate all these notifications in one convenient place, making it easier for students to stay updated without having to check multiple websites daily.

Our Commitment

  • Accuracy: We source all information directly from official websites and clearly indicate the source of each notification.
  • Timeliness: Our automated system checks for updates regularly to ensure you receive notifications as soon as they are published.
  • Transparency: We are an independent platform and clearly state that we are not affiliated with any government board or examination authority.
  • Privacy: We respect your privacy and only collect necessary information to provide you with personalized notifications.

Why Choose GETNOTIFY?

In today's fast-paced educational environment, missing a single notification can have significant consequences. GETNOTIFY helps you:

  • Save time by checking one platform instead of multiple websites
  • Never miss critical deadlines or announcements
  • Stay organized with a centralized notification system
  • Get instant alerts for exams you care about
  • Access historical notifications and announcements

Our Story

GETNOTIFY was born from a simple observation: students often miss important exam notifications because they don't have the time or resources to check multiple official websites daily. We created this platform to solve that problem.
