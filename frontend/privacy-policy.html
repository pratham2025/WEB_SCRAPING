  • ❌ Financial data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide account access
  • Sync your preferences across devices
  • Improve notification accuracy
  • Enhance user experience
  • Maintain security & prevent misuse

We never sell or share your information with third-party advertisers.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Session management

4. Data Storage

All user data is securely stored in Supabase, a GDPR-compliant platform. Supabase uses encrypted storage and JWT-secured authentication.

5. Third-Party Authentication

GETNOTIFY uses Google OAuth for login. We only request basic profile information (name, email, profile photo). We do not post, share, or modify your Google account in any way.

6. Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Account deletion
  • Removal of stored data
  • Export of your user data
  • Logout from all sessions

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: support@getnotify.in

This service is intended for users 13+ years of age.
