GETNOTIFY uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and provide essential functionality. By continuing to browse, you agree to the use of essential cookies as described below.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

GETNOTIFY uses cookies to:

  • Keep your session active - Maintain your login state so you don't have to sign in repeatedly
  • Improve page loading performance - Cache resources to speed up page loads
  • Maintain login state - Remember your authentication status across page visits
  • Store preferences - Remember your saved sources and notification settings

What We Do NOT Use Cookies For

We do not use cookies for:

  • ❌ Advertisements
  • ❌ Behavioral tracking
  • ❌ Selling user data
  • ❌ Cross-site tracking
  • ❌ Third-party marketing

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

  • User authentication (Supabase session cookies)
  • Security features
  • Basic site navigation

These cookies cannot be disabled as they are essential for the website to work.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember your preferences and provide enhanced features:

  • Saved exam sources
  • Notification preferences
  • Display settings

Third-Party Cookies

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage. Supabase may set cookies for session management and security. These cookies are essential for the authentication system to work properly.

We also use Google OAuth for login. Google may set cookies as part of their authentication process. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about their cookie usage.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies

Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website, including your ability to log in and access your account.
