About GETNOTIFY

GETNOTIFY is a real-time educational notification platform that scrapes official sources like CBSE, NTA, and UPSC to keep students informed about results, admit cards, and important updates. Built with FastAPI, Supabase, and a modern brutalist UI, it’s designed to keep Indian students connected to every exam alert that matters.
